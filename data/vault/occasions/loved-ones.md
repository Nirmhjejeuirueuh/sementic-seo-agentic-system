The human-bereavement half of what the source document lists as one
Occasion. Its own note names "Loved Ones" as one of several existing
collections alongside "Pet Memorials" and "Pet Urns" — evidence this was
always two audiences under one tag, not one.

Split out after the keyword join showed pet-loss and human-loss searches
sharing enough vocabulary (mainly "memorial", "loss") that Phase 6.1's
clustering merged them into a single 49-keyword cluster, producing a
page-writing agent output that was 100% pet-focused despite the cluster
containing keywords like "loss of husband gift" and "loss of mother
gift" -- a different audience entirely, with no page serving it.

The aliases here were moved from Memorial / Loss, not duplicated: the
mentor's own keyword list categorises "sympathy gift", "bereavement
gift", "grief gift", "remembrance gift" etc. as "Memorial - Human"
specifically (as opposed to "pet remembrance gift", "pet loss gift"
etc., separately categorised "Memorial - Pet"), so this split follows a
real signal in the source data rather than an invented distinction.
Memorial / Loss keeps "rainbow bridge" (pet-specific in the same
categorisation) and stays the pet-memorial entity.
