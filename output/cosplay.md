## Frequently Asked Questions About Cosplay Figurines

### What is Cosplay?

Cosplay involves a real person in costume, often photographed at an event or during a shoot. While it shares proximity with themes like Anime and Superhero, it is distinct because its subject is a real individual embodying a character.

### What is a cosplay figurine?

A **cosplay figurine** is a collectible that depicts a real person in their own cosplay outfit. These figurines are often based on photographs taken at an event or a dedicated photoshoot, capturing the individual's unique costume and portrayal.

### How are cosplay figurines different from Anime or Superhero figurines?

While **cosplay figurines** may be adjacent to Anime and Superhero themes, they are distinct. The key difference is that a cosplay figurine features a real person in costume, rather than a direct, fictional character from an Anime series or a Superhero comic. They celebrate the individual's embodiment of a character.
