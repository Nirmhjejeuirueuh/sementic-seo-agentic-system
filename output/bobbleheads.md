## What is a Bobblehead?

A bobblehead is a type of collectible figurine characterized by an oversized head connected to its body by a spring or hook. This design allows the head to "bobble" or oscillate when gently touched, giving the figurine its distinctive movement and name. Bobbleheads are often crafted to resemble specific individuals, characters, or mascots, capturing their likeness in a caricatured style. They have a long history as novelty items, gaining significant popularity in sports memorabilia and pop culture, serving as cherished collectibles and personalized gifts.
