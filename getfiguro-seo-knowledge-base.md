## 2. Content Gap Analysis

| Gap Area | Missing Today | Why It Matters for SEO |
|---|---|---|
| Occasions | Birthday, Graduation, Valentine's Day, Christmas/Holiday, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Retirement, Baby Shower, Housewarming, New Baby | These are high-volume, seasonal, recurring search terms ("graduation gift figurine," "christmas custom figurine") not currently targeted by any page. |
| Figurine Type | Sports (general), Model/Hobbyist, Baby, Celebrity/Fan-art, Anime (as a category, not just one product), Family (exists, but not style-crossed) | People search by *what the figurine is of* — "custom baby figurine," "anime figurine of myself" — not just by occasion. |
| Figurine Style | Funky/Pop-vinyl style, Realistic (exists as 1 product, not a collection), Nendoroid (exists as 1 product, not a collection), Anime style (not a collection) | Style is a primary purchase filter — shoppers search "[style] custom figurine" and expect a browsable style collection. |
| Cross-matrix pages | None | Long-tail queries like "anime style baby figurine" or "realistic wedding figurine" currently have no landing page, so traffic defaults to the homepage or is lost to competitors. |

**Core recommendation:** Build out three new top-level collection pillars — **Occasion**, **Figurine Type**, **Figurine Style** — each with its own collection page, then interlink every existing and new product into all three pillars it belongs to (a product can and should appear in an occasion collection, a type collection, and a style collection simultaneously).

## 6. Cross-Matrix Long-Tail Pages (Optional Phase 2)

Once the three pillars exist, the highest-value long-tail pages are combinations of **Style × Occasion** or **Style × Type**, since these match how real buyers refine search queries. Prioritize by search volume before building:

| Combination Example | Suggested URL | Notes |

| Realistic + Corporate | `/collections/realistic-corporate-figurines` | Matches existing corporate product's style |

Only build cross-matrix pages once each has a genuinely distinct product set or copy angle — thin, near-duplicate pages hurt SEO more than they help.

## 7. Standard Page Template (apply to every new collection page)

1. **URL slug:** short, hyphenated, keyword-first (see tables above).
2. **Title tag (≤60 characters):** `[Primary Keyword] | Custom 3D Figurines — Figuro`
3. **Meta description (≤155 characters):** benefit + keyword + CTA, e.g., "Turn any photo into a custom [X] figurine. Hand-finished, made from your photos, shipped worldwide. Design yours today."
4. **H1:** exact match or close variant of the primary keyword.
5. **Intro copy (100–180 words):** naturally worked keyword, answer "what is this," "who is it for," "how customization works."
6. **Product grid:** pull in every existing/future product relevant to that pillar (tag products in the CMS with Occasion, Type, and Style attributes so grids can be dynamically filtered).
7. **"How it works" mini-section:** 3–4 steps (upload photo → choose style/pose → we sculpt & print → shipped to you) — reused as a templated block across all pillar pages for consistency and internal linking.
8. **FAQ block (schema-markup eligible):** 3–5 Q&As specific to that page (e.g., for Baby: "How early can I order before the baby shower?").
9. **Cross-links:** to the other two pillars a product belongs to (e.g., a Realistic-style Baby figurine page links to `/collections/baby-figurines` and `/collections/baby-shower-gifts`).
10. **Image alt text convention:** `Custom [style] [type] figurine for [occasion] — Figuro` (keeps alt text descriptive and keyword-relevant without stuffing).

## 8. Implementation & Technical SEO Notes

- **Avoid duplicate/thin content:** many products will appear on multiple pillar pages — use dynamically pulled product grids from a single source of truth rather than duplicating product descriptions across pages.
- **Canonicalization:** if a product's own page overlaps heavily with a collection's intro copy, keep collection intros short and distinct so they don't compete with product pages in search results.
- **Internal linking:** add an "Occasion / Type / Style" filter or mega-menu in main navigation so all three pillars are crawlable within 2 clicks of the homepage.
- **Schema markup:** use `Product`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` structured data on every new page.
- **Seasonal pages (Christmas, Valentine's, Mother's/Father's Day):** keep the URL live year-round (don't 404 it after the season) — update copy seasonally but preserve accumulated link equity.
- **Trademark/IP caution:** do not use "Funko Pop," named celebrities, or copyrighted characters/franchises in page titles, meta tags, or body copy; use descriptive generic style language instead.
- **Tagging system:** add three CMS metafields to every product — `occasion`, `figurine_type`, `figurine_style` — so any new pillar or cross-matrix page can be auto-populated rather than manually maintained.
